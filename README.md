## Interactive features (Lab 2)

- **Filter the reading list.** In "Reading list", type in the search box or click a genre button (All / Fantasy / Romance) to narrow the list instantly.
- **Add a book.** In "Reading list", fill in the "Add a book" form (title, author, genre) and click "Add book" — the new book behaves exactly like the original seven.
- **Mark a book as read, or remove it.** On any book in "Reading list", click "Mark as read" to strike through its title, or "✕" to remove it.
- **Get inline contact-form validation.** In "Contact", click "Send message" with an empty or invalid field to see an error message appear right next to it, with no page reload.
- **Toggle dark mode.** Click "Dark mode" in the top-right of the header to switch the whole page between light and dark themes.
