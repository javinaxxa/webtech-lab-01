    Projects and achievements

    Digital transformation at Enaex

    During my professional internship in the Supply Management department, I led the shift from a process handled entirely in Excel to a digital workflow: I built a form in Power Apps that replaced the spreadsheet, the data is stored in a SharePoint database, and I automated repetitive tasks with Power Automate, leaving the result visible in Power BI dashboards for the team's decision-making.

    Excel → Power Apps (form) → SharePoint (database) → Power Automate → Power BI

    Strait of Hormuz simulation

    Together with a classmate, I modeled the Strait of Hormuz in Arena to study how the current conflict in the region affects ship traffic. The simulation helped identify the bottlenecks along the strait and estimate their effect on transit times, in a context where real data is scarce and reasonable assumptions have to be made.
